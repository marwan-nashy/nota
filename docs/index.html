FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I try Nota directly in the browser without downloading?

Yes! Nota runs 100% client-side in any modern web browser without needing to download or install any software. Simply click Try Web App to explore your personal workspace, databases, Kanban boards, and templates immediately. All changes are stored locally in your browser and can be exported as Markdown, HTML, or JSON at any time.

Why is Nota so much lighter than traditional note apps?

Traditional workspace apps are built on Electron, which bundles an entire copy of Google Chrome and Node.js with every window, consuming 600MB to 1.2GB of RAM. Nota is built on Tauri v2 with a compiled Rust backend and utilizes your operating system's native Evergreen WebView2, keeping idle RAM strictly under 35MB.

Does Nota require an internet connection?

No. Nota is 100% local-first. All your workspaces, pages, databases, and blocks are saved instantly on your machine with 0ms latency.

Is Nota open-source and free?

Yes, Nota is released under the GNU General Public License v3.0 (GPLv3). It is free to use, modify, and inspect forever.
